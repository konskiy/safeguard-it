Why small businesses need cybersecurity

Attackers do not only look for big companies. Small businesses often have fewer protections, so they are easier to reach.

One bad email can stop your work

A fake email or a bad link is all it takes to lock your files or steal a password.

Your data is your business

Customer lists, invoices and orders are hard to replace. Losing them costs time and trust.

You may not have an IT team

Most owners are busy running the business. Security should not be one more job on the list.

Our services

Six simple services that cover your computers, network, email and data.

Cybersecurity Checks

We look for weak spots in your computers, network and settings.

We review your devices, passwords and Wi-Fi, then send a short report with clear steps to fix each problem.

Data Backup & Recovery

Your important files are copied automatically and can be restored fast.

Backups run on a schedule without you doing anything. If a file is lost or locked, we help you bring back a clean copy.

Account & Email Security

Safer business email and logins, with multi-factor authentication (MFA).

MFA asks for a second proof, like a code on your phone. We set it up and add filters that catch fake and dangerous emails.

Antivirus & Malware Protection

Protection that finds and stops harmful software before it spreads.

We install and manage antivirus on your computers, keep it current and deal with anything it finds.

Software Updates & Security Patches

We keep your programs up to date so known holes are closed.

Updates are checked and installed in a planned way, so your team is not interrupted during the working day.

Employee Security Training

Short, simple lessons that help your team spot scams.

We teach everyday habits: strong passwords, safe links and what to do when something looks wrong. No technical knowledge needed.
